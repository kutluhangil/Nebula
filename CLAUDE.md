# Nebula

Design direction: high-end-visual-design

Chosen 2026-09-07. Agency-grade polish — deliberate type pairing and scale, a
disciplined spacing system, layered depth over flat 1px borders, and a single
accent that carries state. Dark ground is the primary surface; light is a full
peer theme, not an afterthought. Do not mix in another top-level design skill
(minimalist-ui, industrial-brutalist-ui, gpt-taste); the companions in the
global rules — frontend-design, motion-design, dataviz, theme-factory,
design-system, ui-styling — stack freely on top of it.
